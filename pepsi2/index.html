02

14-point inspection

Fourteen Lenses, One Scale, Every Gate

We score every idea on the same 14 lenses before each gate, based on evidence, not confidence. The bar rises at each gate, and the 14th lens writes down what would make you stop before a project starts.

The scale

ScoreLevelWhat it means
1UnknownNo work yet, or major assumptions nobody has checked
2ClaimedA written hypothesis or estimate, with no outside evidence
3ObservedEarly evidence from research, interviews, data or experts
4TestedConfirmed in a proof of technology, proof of concept or pilot in one sector
5ProvenHolds up at scale or across two or more sectors
